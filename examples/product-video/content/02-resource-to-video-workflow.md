# From product resources to video

A product resource directory can contain Markdown or text, PDF and DOCX documents, tables, images, audio, video, and an existing PPTX.

The first step is to select one product resource directory. It immediately becomes the access boundary for the run. The GUI automatically scans the directory and shows the discovered resources.

The content workflow is:

1. Select the Provider and model.
2. Analyze images or rendered PDF/DOCX pages with Vision.
3. Review and approve the Vision report.
4. Generate a structured Draft with outline and narration.
5. Edit and approve the Draft.
6. Build and approve the PPTX and PDF.
7. Generate audio and subtitles. They are automatically accepted after generation and remain available for local review.
8. Build the primary-language, secondary-language, and dual-track videos.
10. Review the local video outputs.

Each explicit approval protects the next gated stage from unreviewed changes. Audio and subtitles are automatically accepted after generation and are provided for local review.
