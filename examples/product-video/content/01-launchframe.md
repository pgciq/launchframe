# LaunchFrame

LaunchFrame is a review-gated workspace for creating multilingual product promotional videos from product resources.

It brings together product documents, images, tables, presentations, narration, subtitles, and video outputs in one local workflow.

The primary language is English. A configurable secondary language can be selected from the Azure Speech Voice catalog.

The workflow is intentionally reviewable:

- resource scan;
- Vision analysis when images or document pages are available;
- LLM Draft generation;
- PPTX and PDF review;
- audio and subtitle review;
- local video review.

The project does not automatically upload or publish product videos. Users download the reviewed local deliverables and publish them through the normal company process.
