# Local review and delivery

The generated files remain in the product project's local output directory.

Typical outputs include:

- generated PPTX;
- presentation PDF;
- primary-language WAV and video;
- secondary-language WAV and video;
- SRT and WebVTT subtitles;
- dual-track video.

The Web GUI provides audio players, live subtitles, subtitle editing, large video previews, WebVTT controls, and an audio-track selector for the dual-track output.

After review, use `Open local output folder` or download the files from the GUI. LaunchFrame does not publish the files automatically.
